HomeTerms of Service

This policy explains how Sur-e-Syahi Shorts Uploader ("the application") handles information. The application is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Sur-e-Syahi YouTube channel to upload its own videos.

YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

When you sign in with Google, the application asks for one permission: youtube.upload. It uses this only to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to your own channel. It does not access your email, contacts, watch history, comments, analytics or any other account data.

How information is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are saved only on the computer where the application runs, in a local file. They are never sent to any server other than Google's. The application has no backend server or database of its own, and collects no analytics, cookies or tracking data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the local computer until they are deleted or access is revoked. You can revoke the application's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes all stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reach us through the Sur-e-Syahi YouTube channel.
